# 🎀 Happy Birthday Muunie

A little birthday website for Malaiqa, built from our memories. It's inspired by [nikitayadav19/HappyBirthdayGF](https://github.com/nikitayadav19/HappyBirthdayGF).

## Pages
1. **index.html**: typewriter greeting, and a "call her" button that cycles her names (baby, muunie, muun, Mylaiqa, my love) while she gets shyer.
2. **reasons.html**: ten reasons, revealed one at a time, each with a real line from our chats.
3. **memories.html**: our timeline from 8 March to 26 September, a photo gallery with love notes, the engagement photo, a small apology, and a cake to blow out that reveals the birthday letter.

Plain HTML, CSS and JS. GSAP is bundled locally (`gsap.min.js`), so it works offline too.

## Run / share
- Open `index.html` in a browser, or
- Enable **GitHub Pages** (Settings → Pages → deploy from branch) and send her the link.
