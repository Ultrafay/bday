Our little timeline

Six months, a hundred thousand messages, a thousand "bye bye muun"s, and still not enough.

💌
8 March

The first hello

I texted you "Hope it's okay reaching out", then panicked and asked twice whether you were okay talking to me. You were so polite, and I had no idea my whole life was about to change.

"Nahi mujhe koi issue nahi hai."No, I don't have any problem with it.
📚
March

Books, "Stutter Island" & 5 Murder

I guessed psychology from your bookshelf, you called me a stranger, I called you a nerd ("the good type, books wali"), and I faked spoilers about a goat dying in The Alchemist. You still don't trust my spoilers.

"Ma ne believe hi nahi karna ab ki baar."I'm not believing you this time.
🕷️
End of March

RIP my Spiderman

Usaid broke my Spiderman, you laughed, and I took his walkie-talkie hostage until he brings a new one. I'm still waiting, by the way.

"Ap ko apna spiderman baacha kar rakhna chahie tha 😂"You should have kept your Spiderman safe.
📞
24 April

The first call

You called, I slept through it. Then I called, and you were so scared because Mursaleen bhai was sitting right next to your phone. The next day he didn't believe you hadn't talked to me, because you were too shy to say anything.

"Unko lag raha hai ky ma jhoot bol rahi ho q ky mujhe sharam arahi hai"He thinks I'm lying because I'm feeling shy.
🌙
2 May

How "muun" was born

I came over with books from the book fair. I heard mumma call you "ammu" and my heart heard "muuu". You asked if you were a cow. No baby, it's short for moon, because that's what you are.

"Muuu, short for mooon 🫢"
💬
15 to 17 May

The secret nickname war

I gave you "mon coeur" and wouldn't explain it. You gave me "jagi" and wouldn't explain either, so I had to ask Claude. And then Spidey was born.

"You don't need to know, it's a nickname, just think it doesn't have a meaning 🫣"
🌸
28 & 29 May

The magic words

A 25-second call from you and a 26-second call from me, the two bravest calls of our lives. I heard it in 4K. You said you weren't picking up again because your nervous system couldn't handle it.

"I'm not mad, I'm just shy 😭"
🍮
30 May

Master chef & her kheer

My family came to dinner and you made kheer. Then we had our cutest fight ever, because you thought I didn't like it. I did. It was perfect. You're my master chef.

🥥
June

Coconuts, songs & "I like us"

If we weren't humans, we'd be two coconuts on a thela together. I sang for you, worried I'd sound bad, and you said it made you smile. You told me muun was becoming your favourite nickname, and that "like" doesn't describe what you feel.

"I don't like changes in my life very much but I'm happy with the change this relation has brought."
☕
30 June & 1 July

Extra sugar & whispered salams

I came to your house and couldn't look up even once ("default settings"). You whispered Allah Hafiz so softly I never heard it, and my chai was extra sweet because you'd told mumma I love meetha.

"You were looking down every time I came 🤣"
😂
July

Mowgli of Surjani Town

You announced our engagement with a status so no more rishtas would come. The Harpic company got a new co-CEO, you woke up to a "nikah certificate prop.docx" and 4 daughters and 6 sons, and your mumma got you engaged to Mowgli.

"Mumma ne meri engagement mougli se Karwa di 🤧"Mumma got me engaged to Mowgli.
🍗
2 August

Movie, KFC & gajray

Our first outing (Mudassir came along). You wore heels so you "weren't small" and I still called you my smol cutie. I picked your hijabs myself. My favourite part was talking to you alone at KFC. It felt just like our WhatsApp.

"Humne pictures lene k ilawa sb kra 🥲"We did everything except take pictures.
🏡
9 August

"It felt like my new family"

You planned the whole visit yourself and gave me a gift in my favourite colour. For the first time I felt like part of your family.

"Can't ask you out on a date but I can do this a little for you… Today was my type of date."
🍢
6 September

Our first real talk, alone

You came to scold me about serious things and I couldn't stop smiling. My angry woman and her shy man. And then you said the line I will keep forever.

"Jiii there was love. There is love. There will be love inshallah."
🎂
26 September

Today: your day

You asked me out for today, weeks ago: ice cream, and me yapping. You dreamt we held hands and tried street food. So today is yours, muunie, every single minute of it.

Pictures of my whole world

I've looked at these more times than I'll ever admit. You told me you rarely smile in photos. Somehow you're still the prettiest thing in every one of them.

Those eyes, baby

The same eyes I couldn't look into when we first met, and honestly still can't without my ears going red. Soft white dupatta, that tiny nose ring, and a look that says "I know you're staring, Ray". Yes. Always.

The queen of the chessboard

Black and white checks, a little pink flower, and that side-eye. It's the "sneaky woman" look, the one before you say something that leaves me speechless. You checkmated my heart months ago, muun. I'm not asking for a rematch.

Soft pink, soft heart

Rose hijab, that pout, and a hand posing like a model who doesn't know she's one. You say you're not photogenic. I say the camera just gets shy around you, the same way I do.

Mylaiqa, my secret calling screen

This one isn't for the world. It only lights up when I call you on your number, so nobody sees it except me: cheek on her hand, that half smile she tries to hide, and a name ringing on my screen. I didn't save you as "Malaiqa", I saved you as MY-laiqa, because you are mine. Every time I call, I get my own little private glimpse of you before you pick up, and I'm the luckiest man in Karachi.

Good vibes only

Us, under a sign that says "good vibes only", as if it needed saying. You in black and gold, so graceful. Me in black, standing just a little apart, too shy to lean in. Every vibe with you is a good one, muunie, even the ones where you're scolding me.

The day you became my fiancée 💍

Our rasam, clipped up here so I can look at it whenever I want. Tap any photo to see it big.

Look at you, Malaiqa. Mustard and gold, pink roses and white motia all around you, mehndi on your hands, and that flower resting on your hijab like it knew exactly who it was for. And me in green, sitting next to the most beautiful girl in the room and trying very hard to act normal.

You didn't see me the first time. The second time you sat next to khala. And the third time, we met straight at our rasam, both of us too shy to even look at each other. In one picture I'm trying to look serious and failing. In another, I'm grinning like I won the whole world, because I did, and you're looking down with that little shy smile you always try to hide. That picture is my favourite. It's us in one frame: me unable to stop smiling, and you, blushing.

Our story didn't start with fireworks. It started with two nervous hearts, a lot of flowers, and you telling me your heart finally felt at peace. That's all I ever want to be for you: peace. The rest (the permit, the "12 daughters and 12 sons" debate we will never settle 😌) will come inshallah.

Ya Allah, keep her smiling, keep her safe, keep her mine, and let the flowers in our garden stay in bloom forever. Ameen. 🤲🌸

P.S. about last night…

So here's my apology, in the only language that suits a bookworm: a whole website instead of a text. I'm learning to be on time, baby, and this is me being early for every birthday of yours for the rest of our lives. 🥺🫶

Twenty looks so good on you

A party hat, silver 2 and 0 balloons, a cake full of candles, and right in the middle the face I'd pick out of a million. That little arrow says "Favorite Person", and it's not lying. You've been my favourite person since the day you whispered your salam so softly I didn't even hear it.

Twenty years of you being you: curious since childhood, a book in one hand, a cat somewhere nearby, and a heart softer than anyone knows. I'm so grateful I get to spend this one with you, muunie, and every birthday after it. Happy 20th, my love. 🎈

You are kind in the quiet ways that matter.
